## Task

A visa centre in Saint Petersburg, by Ploshchad Vosstaniya. Collecting visa documents is stressful and dull, so the site has to calm people down straight away: show where the centre can get you a visa, what it costs and how long it takes, and let people apply or check their status without calling.

## Solution

The site is built on an airport metaphor. The first screen is an LED departures board: a dot-matrix font, yellow lights, and rows with country, visa type, processing time, price and booking status. Next to it are live widgets with the euro rate and local time. Ten destinations are designed as boarding passes with a tear-off stub, and each country has its own page with documents and prices. The passport can be leafed through by swipe or arrows, with country stamps appearing on its pages. First-time visitors see a short intro: a boarding pass, a stamp and a torn stub. Animation is built with GSAP, there are nineteen pages, and prices and times live in one place, so they change without touching the layout.
