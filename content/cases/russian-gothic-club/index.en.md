## Task

A tattoo studio working in engraving, blackwork and gothic styles. Each sketch here is tattooed only once, so the site has to show the available sketches, help people see how a design will sit on the body and bring them to a consultation. At the same time it has to keep the dark mood without turning into a stage set.

## Solution

The site follows old engravings: white lines on near-black, Cologne Cathedral in fog, one blood-red colour for actions and a blackletter logo. On the first visit a star lights up in the centre, a bat flies out of its glow and chases the darkness away. Available sketches hang in ebony frames, taken ones carry a “Taken” stamp. The main idea is the 3D try-on: a sketch wraps right onto a scan of an antique statue, around an arm or a thigh, can be rotated and scaled, and you can book with that sketch straight away. On a phone the try-on opens full screen. Work, sketches, artists, prices, aftercare, the studio, contacts and booking each live on their own page.
