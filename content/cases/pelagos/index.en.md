## Task

Imagine a website for a fictional aquarium that makes visitors feel close to marine life before they arrive. The opening should show animals moving underwater; from there, people should easily learn about each species, choose a hall and plan a visit. Pelagos is a concept: its address, schedule, prices and ticket flow do not represent a real venue.

## Solution

The opening screen becomes a panoramic viewing window. A manta ray approaches from the deep, sardines move as a school, and a sculpted seabed, water light and suspended particles hold them in one scene. The motion responds to the pointer and can be paused. A quieter version is available for reduced motion and less capable devices.

The Depth section descends from sunlit water into darkness. The atlas introduces nine creatures, each with its own page covering habitat, food and behaviour; selected animals can be turned in 3D. A four-hall map helps build a route, a programme lists encounters, and a children's journal tracks discoveries. The visit planner and ticket calculator are demonstrations, while journal marks stay on the visitor's device.
