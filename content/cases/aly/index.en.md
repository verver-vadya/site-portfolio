## Task

Create an identity for a small floral atelier that makes loose, expressive bouquets and decorates events. The website should convey the texture of flowers, help visitors choose a mood and let them try their own combinations. Aly is a fictional brand: this concept demonstrates design and interactions and does not accept orders or payments.

## Solution

Cream paper, large serif type, a red poppy and an original flower logo. The catalogue offers three moods, each with a dedicated bouquet page, ingredients and care instructions. Separate sections introduce the atelier and event styling.

The bouquet builder combines eight flower species, adjustable quantities, wrapping colours and ribbons. Heads sit at different depths, stems meet at the tie and paper folds overlap the plants. Directional light ties the layers together. The composition stays on the visitor’s device and exports as a PNG with its ingredients and an illustrative price.

Pages load without a full refresh, allowing the composition to move between views and a catalogue photo to expand into its product page. The mobile menu grows from its button with a morphing icon; reduced motion has a quieter alternative. The site uses static HTML pages without accounts or a server database.
