## Task

Friday chamber concerts in a former bookbinding workshop on the Petrogradsky side. The hall seats forty, so the site has to show the next dates and the programme quickly and let people sign up without losing the feel of live music.

## Solution

The page is built like a sheet of music: thin staff lines, narrow serif headings, notes “in red pencil”. The listing runs as a list of composers, and every concert has its own window with the programme. The “How the evening goes” block shows the timing step by step, with a line that follows the scroll. On a phone the listing and sign-up work the same as on a computer.
