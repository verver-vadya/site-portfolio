## Task

A salon for short cuts and colour on Malaya Bronnaya Street. It needed to look more upmarket than the neighbours, show prices right away and let people book a specific stylist in a minute.

## Solution

The site feels like the salon itself: the first screen is the room with a brick wall where a lamp lights up, handwritten headings, plaster, terracotta and green velvet. Not one long page but sections: services priced by stylist level, a page for each stylist with their work, a lookbook with an “I want this” button, the salon journal, and contacts that explain how to find the entrance from the courtyard. Booking takes three steps, and the service and stylist are filled in from the page the visitor came from. Everything that repeats lives in one place, so the owner changes prices and stylists without touching the layout.
